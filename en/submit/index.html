How to add a school, course or teacher

The site has no accounts or forms. Schools, courses and teachers send us an email, and our team adds the listing for free.

Three steps

  1. Copy the template below or press "Write an email".
  2. Fill in the details about your school, course or lessons.
  3. Send the email to our address. We check every request and reply within a few days.
