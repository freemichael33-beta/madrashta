Frequently asked questions

What is Modern Assyrian?

Modern Assyrian, also called Sureth, Assyrian Neo-Aramaic or Swadaya, is the living Aramaic language of the Assyrian people. It is spoken in Iraq, Iran, Syria and Turkey and by a large diaspora, and it is written in the Eastern Syriac script from right to left.

Where can I learn Assyrian?

In community and church schools, in language courses and with private teachers. Our directory lists them by region and country, from Europe and the Middle East to the Americas and Australia, and also online courses.

Can I learn Assyrian online?

Yes. Many teachers and courses give lessons online. In the directory, look for listings with the format "Online" or "In person and online".
