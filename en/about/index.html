About the school

Madrashta, the Assyrian language school, helps children and adults learn Sureth and Turoyo, the living Neo-Aramaic languages of the Assyrian people.

Modern Assyrian is spoken by communities in Iraq, Iran, Syria and Turkey and by a large diaspora in Europe, the Americas, Australia, Ukraine, Georgia, Armenia and Russia.

Our directory brings together schools, church and community courses and private teachers, so that every family can find a way to keep the language alive.

The website is available in nine languages, including Assyrian itself.

What we believe in

  • Open access: the directory is free for learners and teachers.
  • Trust: every listing is checked by a person and shows its source.
  • Privacy: we collect as little data as possible.
